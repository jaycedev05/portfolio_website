Case Study
SaaS Analytics

FlowMetrics

Real-time analytics platform for SaaS companies, providing automated reporting, cohort analysis, and revenue forecasting.

The Challenge

The company was using a traditional PostgreSQL-based analytics stack that couldn't keep up with their growth. Dashboard queries were taking 12+ seconds, real-time features were impossible, and their monthly cloud bill for analytics alone exceeded $40K.

My Approach

  • Designed a streaming architecture with Kafka for event ingestion and ClickHouse for columnar storage
  • Built a materialized view layer for common dashboard queries, reducing computation at query time
  • Implemented WebSocket-based real-time updates to eliminate polling
  • Created a custom query optimizer that routes queries to the optimal storage backend

Results

Mobile HealthTech

PulseCare

Cross-platform health monitoring app with real-time vitals tracking, telemedicine, and AI-powered health insights.

The Challenge

Building a consumer health app that meets HIPAA requirements while delivering a premium mobile experience. The app needed to sync data from Bluetooth medical devices in real-time, support offline usage, and integrate with 2,000+ healthcare providers for telemedicine.

My Approach

  • Designed an offline-first architecture with SQLite local storage and background sync
  • Built a custom Bluetooth Low Energy manager for cross-platform device communication
  • Implemented end-to-end encryption for all health data at rest and in transit

Platform Marketplace

VendorHub

Multi-vendor marketplace platform handling $15M+ GMV with real-time inventory and automated fulfillment.

The Challenge

Building a marketplace from scratch that could handle multi-vendor inventory management, split payments, real-time search across 5,000+ vendors, and automated order fulfillment — all while maintaining sub-200ms response times as the platform scaled.

My Approach
